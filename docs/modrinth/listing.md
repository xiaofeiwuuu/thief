**Changelog** (paste):

```markdown
First public release.

- Thieves that visit at night and steal ripe crops and things from chests, then run (and shine for 20 seconds). Bells, iron doors and dogs keep them off.
- Crews: robbers, a lookout, ransom and rescue.
- The magician: copies, reaching into chests, cards, smoke, disguise.
- Rope, whip, binding rack, bounty board, thief tracker, magician's badge and an in-game guide book.
- Tie a thief to a tree, hang it up, or spread it on a rack; lead it to the bounty board for emeralds.
- Commands: /thief ledger (everyone), visit | crew | magician | spawn | debug (operators).
```

4. Last, **Submit for review**.
